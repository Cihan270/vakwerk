# Vakwerk

Your schedule, subjects and handwritten notes in one app for iPad and Apple Pencil. It is inspired by Notability and GoodNotes, with a dashboard that shows your timetable.

## Features

- **Dashboard** with the current or next lecture, today's or this week's schedule, deadlines, flashcards due for review, and new summaries.
- **Per subject**: notes, summaries (typed, handwritten, or made by Claude from Canvas), assignments, and practice.
- **Pencil editor** with pen (pressure-sensitive), highlighter, eraser (whole stroke or precise), and lasso. It has palm rejection: the Pencil writes and your finger scrolls.
- **Smooth writing**: shaky lines are smoothed out.
- **Gestures**: scribble to erase, hold to straighten a shape, circle and hold to select, tap with 2 or 3 fingers to undo or redo, pinch to zoom.
- **Audio synced with ink**: record a lecture while you write, then tap a line to hear what was said at that moment.
- **Flashcards with spaced repetition**: terms and practice questions from summaries become cards automatically.
- **Search** across subjects, notes, summaries, terms and assignments.
- **Offline** and **private**: everything is stored only on your iPad, with backup to Files.

## Installing on your iPad

1. Open the app's URL in **Safari**.
2. Tap Share **⬆︎** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Vakwerk from your home screen.

## Summaries from Canvas

Ask Claude on your laptop "Prepare today's lectures". Claude reads the slides in Canvas and puts `vakwerk-YYYY-MM-DD.json` in your OneDrive under Vakwerk. In the app, tap **Import**. See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the format.
